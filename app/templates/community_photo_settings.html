Contribution conditions

  • Only share photographs you took yourself and have the right to license. Do not submit images downloaded from another website or supplied by someone else.
  • For this first version, shared photographs must not contain identifiable people. Check backgrounds as well as the aircraft.
  • You keep your copyright. Approved photographs are published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  • Anyone may copy, edit, redistribute and commercially reuse them, provided they give appropriate credit, link the licence and identify changes. No endorsement is implied.
  • AirTrack may resize and re-encode a repository copy, remove metadata including GPS, and approve, reject or remove a listing. We do not put an AirTrack ownership watermark on other photographers’ submissions.
  • Opting out stops future submissions. Removing a listing cannot recall downloaded copies or cancel licences already validly granted.
  • Your photographer credit is public. Your contact email, contributor access token and local settings are private. New contributor access needs operator approval; unsuitable submissions may lead to uploads being paused.

Existing images are never replaced. Registration and available MSN/type must match; photographer credit and the licence travel with each download.
